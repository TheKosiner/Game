GlitchSoul privacy policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

GlitchSoul is a free RPG played in the browser (glitchsoul.com) and as an Android app. This policy explains what data we process about you, why, and what your rights are.

1. Controller

The data controller is the creator of GlitchSoul. Contact for anything about your data: thekosiner@gmail.com.

2. What we process

  • Account: email address, player name, account creation date and the date you accepted the rules. Passwords are handled by Firebase Authentication (Google) — we never see them.
  • Game progress: hero, level, items, gold and gems, quests, fight results, guild membership.
  • Public profile: player name, hero name, portrait, level, equipment and PvP rating — visible to other players in rankings, guilds and the lobby.
  • Content you create: global, guild and lobby chat messages, in-game mail, guild descriptions. Other players see them (everyone, guild members, or the recipient respectively).
  • Moderation: reports (who reported whom, a copy of the reported message), the players you block, and a mute if a moderator applies one.
  • Purchases: when you buy gems — order ID, product, date and payment confirmation. We never receive card details: payments are handled by Stripe (website) or Google Play (the app from the Play Store).
  • Technical data: app error reports (error message, stack trace, page path, browser/device, game version, player ID and name) — used only to fix bugs.
  • On your device: a copy of your save, your language and tutorial progress. Reminders (e.g. a quest finishing) are scheduled locally on the device — we collect no push tokens.

We show no ads, use no analytics or tracking tools, and never sell data.

3. Purposes and legal bases

  • Running your account and the game, including its social features — performance of a contract (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)).
  • Handling purchases and refunds — contract and legal obligations (Art. 6(1)(b) and (c)).
  • Security, moderation, preventing cheating and fixing bugs — legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f)).

4. Service providers

  • Google (Firebase: sign-in, database, server functions; Google Play; Google Fonts). The database is in the EU; server functions run in the USA, under Standard Contractual Clauses and the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.
  • GitHub — hosting of glitchsoul.com and the APK file (server logs such as IP addresses).
  • Stripe — payments on the website.
  • Discord — only if you choose to visit our Discord server.

5. Retention

  • Account, progress, profile and your messages — until you delete your account (anytime, see account deletion).
  • Purchase confirmations — as long as tax law and refund handling require; after account deletion they are linked only to a pseudonymous ID.
  • Reports by other players about your account — until resolved, at most 12 months.
  • Error reports — until the bug is fixed, at most 12 months.

6. Your rights

You have the right to access, rectify and erase your data, to restrict processing, to data portability, and to object to processing based on legitimate interests. Write to thekosiner@gmail.com. You can also delete your account yourself in the game: Hero → Account → Delete account. You may lodge a complaint with your data protection authority.

7. Age

The game is not intended for anyone under 16 (it has chat with other players and a simulated roulette played with in-game gold).

8. Security

Connections are encrypted (HTTPS). Database security rules let each player change only their own data, and operations that need trust (purchases, rewards) run on the server.

9. Changes

We will announce significant changes to this policy in the game. The current version is always at this address.
