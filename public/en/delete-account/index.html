Deleting your GlitchSoul account

Last updated: 8 November 2026

Delete your account in the game

  1. Sign in to the game (browser or Android app).
  2. Open the Hero tab and scroll to the Account section.
  3. Choose Delete account, type your player name and confirm.

The account is deleted immediately and this can't be undone.

Can't sign in?

Email thekosiner@gmail.com from the address linked to the account, with the subject GlitchSoul account deletion and your player name. We will delete the account within 30 days and confirm by email.

What is deleted

  • Your sign-in account (email and password) and player name.
  • Your hero and entire save, public profile and ranking entries.
  • Your chat messages, mail sent and received, guild invites, PvP and casino history.
  • Your guild membership (a leader's role passes to an officer or the longest-serving member; a guild with no members left is disbanded).
  • Your reports, block list, unclaimed purchased gems and error reports.

What may be kept

  • Purchase confirmations (order ID, product, date) — required by tax law and for handling refunds; linked only to a pseudonymous ID.
  • Reports by other players about your account — until resolved, at most 12 months.
