GlitchSoul community rules

Last updated: 8 November 2026

By creating an account you accept these rules. They exist to keep the game fair and friendly for everyone.

Respect

  • No harassment, threats, hate speech, discrimination or sexual content.
  • Don't post anyone's personal data, including your own (addresses, phone numbers, etc.).
  • No spam, advertising, scam links or attempts to obtain passwords.
  • Player, hero and guild names must follow these rules too.

Fair play

  • No bots or automation, no modifying the game or saves, no exploiting bugs instead of reporting them.
  • One person — one account for competitive play. No trading accounts or items for real money.
  • The casino roulette uses only in-game gold, which can't be bought or cashed out — it has no real-world value.

Reporting and blocking

  • Every message from another player has a ⋮ button: Report sends it to the moderators, Block player hides their messages and mail.
  • Unblock players in Hero → Account.

Moderation

Moderators may remove content, mute an account (no chat or mail) or, in serious cases, delete it. Contact about a decision: thekosiner@gmail.com.

Purchases

Gems are virtual items with no cash value. Refunds are handled by the store you bought from (Google Play or Stripe) under its terms. Gems can't buy random rewards or any advantage you can't earn by playing.
